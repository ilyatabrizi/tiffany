3 · Teddy coat colours (C5)

Read as four colours: سفید کرمی (creamy white, the one in the photos), زرشکی، مشکی، شکلاتی. It could also be five, with سفید and کرمی separate.

Choices I made

Read these once

  • Posts that repeat a piece word for word are listed once: bandana, sweatshirt and trousers (C4 = C5), the white/pastel-pink cap (C2 = C7), the five-colour cap (C13 = C15), the sneakers (C9 + C14), and the five-colour shirt (C9, C13, C14, C15, C19).
  • Merged because the photos or the details match: the oversize coat (C17 + C19), the green/cream jeans (C10 + C24), the fleece trousers (C4/C5 + C12), and two bandanas worn twice (C8 + C17, C9 + C16).
  • Kept apart because a detail differs: the C16 shirt (lists سفید in place of صورتی), the C10 plain shirt (four other colours), and the C15 dors trousers (a different colour list). Tell me if any of these is really the same piece.
  • «تک رنگ» pieces take the colour visible in the photo (for example مشکی for the oiled leather jacket). Each «تک رنگ تک مدل» bandana is its own piece, named by its print.
  • Sizes: std, فری and تک سایز become one size; jeans 34 to 42 (41 → 42, as you said); sneakers 37 to 40.
  • Brands go only where the caption names them: KARL LAGERFELD, MARC JACOBS, original Zara.
  • Names are short and distinct, built from the caption words plus what the photo shows (for example «بافت پشم یقه‌بلند»). English and Turkish names are translations of those.
